For as long as I can remember,
music was present in my life.

Long before I began learning how to create with code,
I was learning how to create with sound.

A voice joining a choir.
Breath becoming a note.
Hands discovering patterns in rhythm.

I realized music wasn't only heard.
It also had motion.

It moves through a room.
Through a body.

A pulse beneath your feet.
A vibration against your chest.
Rhythm pulling movement from stillness.

Sound becomes feeling.
Feeling becomes experience.
A thousand souls in a room moving as one.

Years later, I began learning a new language.

One line. One mistake. One lesson at a time.

Lost in syntax, I began to notice
what existed between the syllables.

Fragmented stories, memories, rhythm, emotion.

Patterns written in different languages
beginning to move as one.

People within the music.
Music within the code.
Elements collide,
and community begins to form
in the output of creativity.

The idea that code
could have rhythm too.

Movement. Response. Interaction.

Two languages learned years apart
began speaking to each other.

Mapping the places inside me
my mind hasn't learned to access.

Ideas I can see
yet cannot set in motion.

I have no idea where this path may end.

I only know I made it further today
than I was able to yesterday.
